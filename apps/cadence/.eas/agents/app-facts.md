## About Cadence

Cadence is a task app for things you do on a loop, such as "water the plants every 3 days". It keeps all data on the device. The app is a fresh install, so it has no tasks.

- The app opens on onboarding. It has three screens. Press Next, Next, then Done. Leave the sync switch on the last screen off. Then the Tasks screen opens.
- The Tasks screen shows starter tasks, such as "Water the plants, every 3 days". Press one to add it, or press "New task" to make your own.
- Stats and Settings are buttons at the top of the Tasks screen.
- Do not turn on sync. Do not sign in to iCloud or Google.
- Do not send feedback from Settings.
- Type only made-up text, such as "Test task". Never type an email address, a name, or a password.
