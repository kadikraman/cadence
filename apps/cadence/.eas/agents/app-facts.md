## About Cadence

Cadence is a task app for things you do on a loop, such as "water the plants every 3 days". It keeps all data on the device. The app is a fresh install, so it has no tasks.

- The app opens on onboarding. It has three screens. Press Next, Next, then Done. Leave the sync switch on the last screen off. Then the main screen opens.
- The first two onboarding screens teach how to add the Cadence widget. They show pictures of the home screen, the launcher menu, and app icons. These pictures are part of Cadence, not the real launcher. A row of three dots and a Next button at the bottom show that Cadence is open.
- The onboarding titles on iOS are "Find Cadence on your Home Screen", "Touch and hold the icon", and "Sync with iCloud".
- The onboarding titles on Android are "Touch and hold your home screen", "Search for Cadence", and "Sync with Google Drive".
- The main screen has the title "Tasks" on iOS and "Cadence" on Android. On Android, "Tasks" is a tab label on that screen. The main screen shows starter tasks, such as "Water the plants, every 3 days". Press one to add it, or press "New task" to make your own.
- Stats and Settings are buttons at the top of the main screen.
- Do not turn on sync. Do not sign in to iCloud or Google.
- Do not send feedback from Settings.
- Do not press "Load sample data" in Settings. It replaces all data.
- Type only made-up text, such as "Test task". Never type an email address, a name, or a password.
