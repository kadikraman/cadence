Check that a new user can finish onboarding and reach the Tasks screen.

1. The app opens on onboarding. Save a screenshot of each onboarding screen.
2. Press "Next" on the first two screens.
3. On the last screen, check that the sync switch is off. Leave it off. Press "Done".
4. Check that the main screen opens with the starter tasks, such as "Water the plants". Its title is "Tasks" on iOS and "Cadence" on Android. Save a screenshot.
5. Send the app to the background with home. Then open the app again. Check that the main screen opens, not onboarding. Save a screenshot.

Out of scope for this lane: do not add a task, do not turn on sync, and do not open Settings.

Pass condition: onboarding has three screens, ends on the main screen, and does not show again when the app opens again.
